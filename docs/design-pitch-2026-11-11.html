VertiStrat Solutions LLP · website design pitch · 11 Nov 2026

Make the site prove what the firm says.

The site already says the right thing: policy desk to plant floor, across manufacturing, IT services, biofuels and energy, and investment facilitation. What it does not yet do is show the evidence behind that claim in the first screen. This pitch proposes six changes that move the evidence to the front and remove what makes a cautious buyer hesitate.

Where the site stands today

  • StructureHome, About, three practice pages, Insights, Trade ideas, one contact section.
  • Evidence on hand501 articles in a searchable library, 5 research themes, 20 featured pieces, a most-read list.
  • LookBento tiles, electric blue with an amber highlight, Host Grotesk and Instrument Sans, light and dark modes.
  • Gaps a buyer seesLLPIN and registered office still read "[placeholder]" in the footer. The disclaimer and trade-ideas link carry placeholder text.
  • Enquiry pathA Google Form embedded in a 1,000 to 1,150 px tall frame.
  • Read fromThe live site and its source on 10 Nov 2026.

Six moves

Today

The hero makes a claim

"From the policy desk to the plant floor" is followed by a generic factory line drawing and four tiles with no content behind them.

Change

Open with proof beside the promise

Keep the headline. Replace the line drawing with a live strip: the three newest research pieces with dates, the article count, and one data point from each practice.

  • Pulls from the existing library, so it updates when the blog does.
  • A visitor sees recent, dated work before scrolling.
Today

Trust details are unfinished

The footer shows "LLPIN: [placeholder]" and "Registered office: [placeholder]". The disclaimer asks to be replaced.

Change

Finish the credibility block before any outreach

Fill LLPIN, registered office and the regulatory disclaimer, and move them into a short "About the firm" block that is also linked from the footer.

  • Needs your registration details; I will not invent them.
  • Blocks nothing else, but every outreach email links here.
Today

Navigation is flat

Six links plus Contact, with practices, insights and trade ideas on one level.

Change

Group by what the visitor wants to do

Services (three practices), Research (insights, trade ideas), About, and one persistent "Talk to us" button.

  • Mobile menu becomes a proper slide-in panel with Escape to close and focus handling.
  • The current page is marked in the nav.
Today

Contact is a long embedded form

The enquiry form sits in a 1,000 to 1,150 px frame loaded from Google, with a link out and an email address underneath.

Change

Ask four questions, promise a reply time

Name, company, what you are planning, and a contact route. Show the response time you can keep next to the button. Keep Google Forms as the backend if you want the same inbox.

  • Fewer fields raise completion.
  • Needs a decision on the reply-time promise.
Today

Insights sits beside the services, not inside them

"Discuss this" links go to the same contact section whatever you were reading.

Change

Let each article open the right conversation

Each practice page lists its three most relevant articles, and every article ends with a prefilled enquiry for that topic.

  • The theme re-sort this week makes the matching more accurate.
  • Gives you a record of which research produces enquiries.
Today

Little shows the work behind the advice

No client names or case summaries yet, which is correct while there are none to share.

Change

Add "How an engagement runs" with a sample

A one-page walk-through of a typical project, using a published piece such as the IEM state analysis as the worked example, labelled as research and not as a client result.

  • No invented clients or results.
  • Swap in real engagements when you have permission to name them.

Shipped this week

  • Page follows the phone's text-size setting in Chrome and Edge
  • Smooth page-to-page transitions, off for visitors who ask for less motion
  • 501 articles re-sorted into six themes; 20 featured articles refreshed
  • Guide tiles match whole words, Approvals tile widened

Next
