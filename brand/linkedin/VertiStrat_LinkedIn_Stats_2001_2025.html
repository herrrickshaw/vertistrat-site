What it shows
• Follow-through rose from about 1 in 5 (2001–06) to about 1 in 3 (2016–19).
• Since 2024 more plants are reported started than new plans filed.
• 2025 Part A filings are the lowest of the five years. It was also the year of the April size threshold and the October move to NSWS.
Sources: A: DPIIT monthly IEM lists 2001–24, parsed by VertiStrat (55,781 memoranda counted once; a lower bound on filings, e.g. the report counts 1,489 for 2021 against 976 here).
