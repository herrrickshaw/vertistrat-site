1
Detect
The system: reads DPIIT IEM filings, PARIVESH clearances and tenders, then logs each project once.
Speeds up: finding who is building what, and where.
2
Qualify
The system: links Part A to clearance to Part B, sizes the ticket in ₹ crore, flags stalled projects.
Speeds up: choosing which projects to approach first.
3
Reach
The system: keeps contact, past conversations and drafted outreach against the same project record.
Speeds up: first contact, with the project facts already in hand.
4
Advise
The system: sites and feedstock, partners, approvals, project report and lender-ready finance case.
Speeds up: turning a conversation into scoped work.
5
Track and publish
The system: updates the tracker and turns the findings into graphics and posts.
Speeds up: the next lead, from what we have already learned.
Why integrate · One record, no re-keying between steps, and each job feeds the next. Limits: matching filings is imperfect, and every message is reviewed by a person before it is sent.
Sources: DPIIT Annual Reports (Part A filings, ₹ crore); PARIVESH 2.0 EC register, counted by VertiStrat (proposals filed 2022 to Oct 2026); DPIIT IEM company lists 2001–24, Part A to Part B linkage by memo number or company name (a lower bound; no Part B found is not the same as abandoned).
