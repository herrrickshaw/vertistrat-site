What it shows · Filings fell from about 2,100 a year to about 1,180, but proposed value rose, so the average filing is now over three times larger. In 2025 actual investment (₹5.83 lakh crore) passed new proposals (₹5.30 lakh crore).
Source: DPIIT Annual Reports 2018-19 to 2023-24 (Appendices III and V) and 2025-26; latest report used where a year was revised (e.g. 2018 Part A: 2,754 became 2,173). 2019 Part B includes ₹15.2 lakh crore from Gujarat (434 filings), probably an outlier; the ticket in C is Part A only. 2025 is to December. Proposals and actual figures are different projects, not a conversion. Company-list counts are lower than report totals.
