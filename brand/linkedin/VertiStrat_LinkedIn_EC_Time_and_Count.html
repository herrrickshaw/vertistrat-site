What it shows
• Delays past the limit grew from 86 to 316 days in 2011–14. Central averages then fell from over 150 days (2019) to under 70 (2022).
• 96% of the 56,417 ECs on the register are state grants. Central grants stay near 600–700 a year.
• Half of all proposals are re-appraisals, transfers or expansions, not new projects.
