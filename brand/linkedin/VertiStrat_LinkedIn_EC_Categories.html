What it shows
• Industrial ECs filed in 2023–25 ran at about 870–960 a year, steady while mining volume swung.
• Chemicals, metals and distilleries make up about 77% of industrial ECs with a stated activity.
• Gujarat alone holds 3 in 10, ahead of Maharashtra and Odisha.
